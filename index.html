Remorse Chess

Play Remorse Chess free in your browser. Every piece rolls a die on its first capture. It can freeze, lose the ability to capture, stay unaffected, or earn a bonus move. A friendly bishop can absolve a piece from any adjacent square.

Find an online match, invite a friend, play on one device, or practice against a bot.
