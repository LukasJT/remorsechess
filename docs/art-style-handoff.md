# Remorse Chess art redesign handoff

Baseline observed in the working tree on November 24, 2026. This is a visual redesign of the existing Remorse Chess product. Treat every route, control, feature, and conditional state below as a preservation requirement. Use the live site and the current working tree as your baseline. The working tree contains deployed changes beyond the latest Git commit.

## Instructions for the AI doing the redesign

- Inspect the repository before editing. Read any applicable repository instructions, check `git status --short`, and trace each screen from the navigation through its markup, event handlers, state, and API. Read the existing styles and open representative desktop and mobile views. Preserve local modifications and new files, including assets.
- Change the art direction, typography, layout, icons, pieces, motion, and styling as requested. Preserve the actual game, accounts, ratings, data, settings, visibility rules, URLs, SEO, and Adsterra integrations. Keep existing actions discoverable when reorganizing the layout. Refactor markup only with the related selectors, handlers, and focus behavior accounted for.
- Search for `data-do`, `data-square`, `data-pref`, `data-tab`, `data-scientist`, `data-control`, `data-friend-action`, form IDs, links, dialog handling, and disabled/hidden states. Read the landing, board, account, and worker paths in `src/client.ts` and `src/worker.ts`. The controls are conditional, so a single screenshot will miss many of them.
- Preserve native button, link, input, select, dialog, and disclosure semantics. Keep clear keyboard focus, arrow-key board navigation, screen-reader square names and status text, mobile tap targets, drag/drop, scroll behavior, and reduced-motion behavior. Keep color-independent indications of selection, turn, piece state, legal moves, and unavailable bots.
- Validate in a local or isolated test environment and review both desktop and phone widths. Production deployment and paid service changes require the site's existing authorization. The project uses Vite and Cloudflare Workers with Durable Objects. The ad iframe Worker deploys separately. Keep authentication, authorization, secrets, privacy boundaries, and Worker bindings intact.

**Rules contract.** `src/rules.ts`, `src/match.ts`, the Rules tab, and `public/rules/index.html` define the mechanics. On a piece's first capture it rolls exactly once, including after absolution or promotion. **1 Devastated** freezes the piece; it self-captures unless saved by the end of its owner's third subsequent turn. **2–3 Guilt-ridden** can move to empty squares but cannot capture until saved. **4–5 Unaffected** plays normally. **6 Psychopath** can get one immediate extra move after capturing on the first move of a turn. The same piece must take a legal bonus move when one exists under the current rules. A bonus move cannot itself chain another bonus. At three captures, **Bloodlust** restricts that piece to captures, including friendly pieces, subject to king safety. A friendly bishop can absolve an eligible guilty or devastated piece from any of the eight adjacent squares, including diagonals. Absolution consumes a turn and leaves both pieces on their squares. A legal bonus capture of the enemy king wins immediately. Preserve check, checkmate, stalemate, castling, en passant, promotion, draws, clock expiry, and the versioned behavior of legacy games. See the engine for exact special-case legality.
