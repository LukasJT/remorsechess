Remorse Chess Support

Sign in on a new device

On iPhone or iPad, open Online and sign in with the account you already use at RemorseChess.com. Your ratings and games are tied to that account. Open Online options and choose Profile and game history to find them.

Get back into your account

If you linked an email address, use the email sign-in link or password reset in the sign-in screen. If you use a passkey, choose the passkey from the device or provider where you saved it.

Delete your account

Sign in and open Profile. Choose Delete account and follow the confirmation steps. See the Privacy Policy for what happens to shared replays.

Report a problem

Report a bug on GitHub. Issues are public. Include your device, app or browser version, and steps to reproduce the problem. Keep passwords, sign-in links, and personal account information private.
