Remorse Chess History

Remorse Chess began with a simple rule. After a piece captures, it has to live with what it did. A friendly bishop can restore its ability to capture.

Original variant by Bardelo Bardalini (@bardelo_bardalini).

The first Remorse Chess TikTok

Bardelo Bardalini introduced Remorse Chess on TikTok in November 2026. In the original demonstration, Bardalini puts a marker under a piece that has just captured. The piece can still move to an empty square, but guilt stops it from taking another piece. A bishop can absolve it by spending a turn beside it.

This changes what happens after a trade. A rook can win a piece and still lose its ability to threaten another capture. Spending a turn on absolution restores that threat, but gives the opponent time to move. Those two decisions drive the original variant. Watch Bardalini’s original Remorse Chess TikTok.

Psychos and devastation

Bardalini followed up with Remorse Chess: Psychos & Devastation, a video exploring other reactions a piece could have to a capture. Its title names two of the outcomes that players now encounter alongside guilt. Watch Bardalini’s follow-up on TikTok.

The original idea also reached readers who found the game through chess and pop-culture coverage. Neatorama described the marker and bishop rule, and Geeks Are Sexy shared the demonstration and explained why guilt changes the value of a capture. Neatorama coverage · Geeks Are Sexy coverage.

How the browser edition plays

RemorseChess.com is an independently developed, free browser adaptation of Bardalini’s variant. Here, a piece rolls one six-sided die on its first capture. Guilt preserves the original move restriction, while a devastated piece freezes, an unaffected piece keeps playing normally, and a psychopath can earn a bonus move after capturing.

On this site a bishop can heal an eligible piece from any neighboring square, including a diagonal. A devastated piece has three of its own side’s subsequent turns to be healed. A psychopath that reaches three captures gains bloodlust and can only move by capturing. The full browser rules explain those mechanics with worked examples.
