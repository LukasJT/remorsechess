Remorse Chess

Privacy Policy

This policy covers RemorseChess.com and the Remorse Chess app for iPhone and iPad.

Offline play and online accounts

Offline play in the app stores your settings and local progress on your device. Online play uses cookies to keep you signed in and to recognize your seat in a game. If you make an account, we store your player name and ID, the sign-in information you choose to provide, and any profile details you add.

Email sign-in stores a verified email record. Passwords are stored as password hashes. For passkey sign-in we store the public credential. Your device or passkey provider keeps the private key. If you use GitHub sign-in where available, we receive your GitHub account ID and username.

Games and public information

We use account and game records to provide ratings, matchmaking, puzzles, game history, friends, and preferences. Your player name can appear in rankings and live games. Signed-in players can see details you add to your profile. Friends you accept can see when you are online. Anyone with a game link can view the game.

Providers

Cloudflare hosts the online service and processes request data to operate and protect it. Resend delivers sign-in and password emails. The website requests fonts from Google Fonts. When sponsor banners are enabled in the website, the banner connects to the Adsterra network. The iPhone and iPad app excludes those banners.

Account deletion

Sign in, open Profile, and select Delete account. In the iPhone and iPad app, open Online and choose Profile and game history in Online options. A recent sign-in and typed confirmation are required. The site signs you out when it accepts the request and gives you a page where you can check its progress.

Deletion removes your account, credentials, profile, and personal rating and game history. Your opponents retain their own results. Shared replays retain the moves and dice rolls and replace your name with “Deleted player.” We retain a technical marker to prevent an old sign-in from recreating your deleted account.
